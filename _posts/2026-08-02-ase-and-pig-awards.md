We have a few exciting updates to share!

Two of our papers have been accepted to **ASE 2026**, to be held in Munich, Germany 🇩🇪:

- **[Disassembly-Augmented Root Cause Analysis for C/C++ Segmentation Faults in SAP HANA](/publications/jung2026disassembly)** (Industry Showcase Track) is joint work with SAP Labs Korea and SAP. The paper identifies a key bottleneck in LLM-based root cause analysis for C/C++ crashes and shows that providing disassembly information about the faulting instruction can improve RCA performance.
- **[Is Three the Magic Number? An Empirical Evaluation of LLM-Based Repair Loops](/publications/kiecker2026three)** (NIER Track) is joint work with Humboldt-Universität zu Berlin. It takes a closer look at repair loops, which is a common component of many LLM-based software engineering frameworks, and studies how many repair iterations are actually useful.

We are also delighted that **[Pig: Leveraging Large Language Models for Python Library Migrations](/publications/kang2026pig)** received both the **ACM SIGSOFT Distinguished Paper Award** and the **Distinguished Artifact Award** at FSE 2026 🏆🏆

Congratulations to all the authors and collaborators! 🎉
